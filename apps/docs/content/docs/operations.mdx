# Operations

Production deployments use immutable images and explicit trust roots. Before
accepting traffic, record the aggregate and recursive submodule SHAs, pass every
release gate, verify the escrow runtime hash and EIP-712 domain, verify the chain
ID and Boltz decoder trust roots, require Cashu NUT-11/NUT-09 capabilities and
active keyset horizons, and review the known limitations.

Keep long-term Nostr keys, Cashu seeds, EVM keys, provider credentials, bearer
proofs, and sealed settlement plaintext out of images, persistent browser
storage, public events, logs, metrics, and release artifacts. Only a short-lived
NIP-46 client authorization may be session-scoped in the browser. Back up only
journals and the minimum versioned recovery records under operator-managed
encryption and access control, and rehearse restoration. Proof payloads are
sealed, but journal metadata is not encrypted by the library.

Monitor stale operations, refund failures, contract or chain mismatches, nonce
failures, NUT-09 recovery failures, relay publication failures, and journal
decryption failures. Reconcile every terminal event with one durable receipt.
Resume ambiguous work with its original operation ID after checking authoritative
chain or mint state; never blindly resubmit.

Disable new work before rollback. Never roll back across an incompatible escrow
domain, journal schema, or trust policy. For a suspected secret disclosure, stop
affected workers, revoke credentials, rotate signers/owners, move funds through
the nonce-bound path, preserve sanitized evidence, and follow `SECURITY.md`.

The complete checklist and recovery procedures also ship in the source tree as
`docs/operations.md`.
