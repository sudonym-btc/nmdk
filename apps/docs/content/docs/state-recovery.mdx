# State and recovery

Operations progress through prepared, externally created, funded, settled,
publication pending, and complete states. Each durable transition happens
before the next external effect. Concurrent workers need atomic
create-if-absent or compare-and-set semantics.

Cashu stores mint/unit, derivation version and index, quote ID, fingerprint,
status, and public receipts. Terminal records do not retain proofs. EVM stores
chain and configured contract identity, account index, swap/provider ID,
transaction or user-operation hashes, fingerprint, confirmations, and public
receipts—never private keys or preimages.

Completed-operation tombstones and signed settlement outboxes are never aged
out automatically. Operators may archive them only after the complete financial
and dispute horizon while retaining immutable event and external receipt
references.

Startup reconciles authoritative mint/provider/chain state. Recovered payments
produce the same publication-ready proof and durable outbox item as an
uninterrupted payment.

Cashu bids carry buyer-signed exact promotion and 100%-refund swap commitments.
The arbiter cannot redirect their outputs. Lost mint responses restore the same
outputs through NUT-09, and buyer refund proofs are delivered only inside a
whole-proof sealed payload.
